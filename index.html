I work in the Continuous Integration Support team (SIC), automating CloudOps operations and managing infrastructure across AWS, Azure and Google Cloud.

  • CloudOps: automating secret and certificate operations, rolling deployments in ECS and Azure Container Apps, scaling, cache invalidations and bulk operations selected by tags through workflows and AWS Systems Manager.
  • IaC and authentication: managing infrastructure and remote Terraform states, including imports and unlocks; configuring OIDC in AWS and Azure.
  • Migrations: moving on-premises Oracle databases to Oracle and Aurora PostgreSQL in AWS with DMS and scripts, including CLOB, BLOB and LONG RAW data. Preparing runbooks and tracking timings for production migrations.
  • Google Cloud: developing reusable, configurable templates for Cloud Run, databases and load balancers.
  • FinOps: analysing consumption and potential savings with Apptio.
